Module 1: Classroom Instruction - Fundamentals of the No-Drill System

Welcome to the Fixyachips Global Solutions training academy. In this video-on-demand series, the first video teaches you the fundamentals of the Fixyachips No-Drill System. NO FLUFF, NO EXTRANEOUS filler to take up your time—only direct explanations of how this system works and how you implement the techniques.

Modules 2 through 18 cover actual, real stone damages being repaired so that you can see exactly how to put into action these trade secret techniques for you to practice.

Keep in mind: when I learned in 1982 from Mike Fine, he showed me twice how to do it, and I had to practice approximately a dozen times before I was fixing windshields for him that summer! IT SHOULDN'T TAKE YOU VERY LONG TO MASTER THE BASICS OF THESE TECHNIQUES, but your skill level will increase the more you do it!!

This course is designed to be a "hands on" VOD instructional course so that you can watch the content and then practice, then come back to the course to refresh your mind, and go practice again. You've been given 30 days of access to complete the course, which is way, way, way more time than you need. I say that because I learned over a period of 2 days! And I was repairing windshields for Mike that first week. You shouldn't need any more time than that to learn this amazing art and science.
