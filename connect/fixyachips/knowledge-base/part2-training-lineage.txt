BUSINESS MODEL & FINANCIAL FREEDOM

Q: How profitable is windshield repair as a business?
A: Each repair uses only a few dollars of resin and supplies while charging $70 to $95 or more. You can complete two to three mobile repairs per hour as a one-person operation. Commercial accounts with dealerships, rental agencies, and delivery fleets provide steady recurring weekly income. In economic downturns demand increases because drivers repair instead of spending $800 or more on replacements.

Q: What are the startup costs?
A: Unlike full glass replacement businesses that need warehouses, glass racks, and heavy delivery trucks, windshield repair requires no inventory storage, no ADAS calibration rigs costing $20,000 to $50,000, and no heavy lifting. All materials fit in a portable case and you can operate out of a fuel-efficient sedan.

Q: Is there ongoing demand year-round?
A: Yes. Gravel trucks, highway construction, and seasonal temperature swings create steady work all 12 months. Freeze-thaw cycles in winter are particularly active seasons because unsealed chips spread rapidly in cold weather.

Q: How do I handle insurance billing?
A: The Fixyachips Method training covers navigating insurance claims effortlessly, guiding customers through three-way insurance calls so their deductible is waived, and educating drivers on their legal right to choose their own repair specialist rather than being steered by an insurer.

Q: Can I run this business solo?
A: Absolutely. The entire operation scales as a one-person business. Just as the original lineage passed from one master to the next, the model is designed to be run independently with zero franchise royalties keeping 100 percent of your profits in your pocket.
